# Example data

## U-87MG glioblastoma cells (real data)

Folders `u87-apoptosis` and `u87-cell-cycle`.

Hassan, S. N., Mohamed Yusoff, A. A., Idris, Z., Mohd Redzwan, N., Ahmad, F. (2026).
*Raw FCS data from U-87MG ATCC cells treated with temozolomide, azithromycin, and/or
doxycycline*. Mendeley Data, V1. https://doi.org/10.17632/d4r5h95356.1

Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

U-87MG cells were treated for 48 h with temozolomide (TMZ), azithromycin (AZI), doxycycline
(DOXY) or azithromycin with doxycycline, at their IC50; NC is the untreated control. The data
were acquired on a BD FACSCanto II.

Changes: CytoScope ships a subset of the dataset, unmodified: the annexin V-FITC / PI tubes of
biological replicate 2 (with its unstained and single-stain controls) and the DNA content
tubes of biological replicate 1 (without the bead and standard tubes).

## Cadmium dose series (simulated data)

Folder `cadmium`. These files are **not real measurements**: they were generated by
`com.bioinfo.cytoscope.tools.SimulatedDoseSeries` to illustrate a dose-response analysis
(mouse splenocytes, cadmium chloride 0 to 50 µM, 24 h, annexin V-FITC / PI, 3 replicates per
dose, with unstained and single-stain controls). The keyword `$SRC` of each file says so.
